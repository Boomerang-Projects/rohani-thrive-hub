# Rohani Wellbeing Solutions

Create a premium, modern, minimalist website for a personal brand and company called:

ROHANI
Peace of Mind

Brand Positioning:
Rohani is a psychology-based wellbeing and human development company founded by a psychologist. The company combines evidence-based psychology, mindfulness, emotional intelligence, self-leadership and human development to help individuals, leaders and organizations thrive.

The brand should feel professional enough for global corporations, while remaining warm, human, grounded and inspiring.

Avoid any "guru", "new age", overly spiritual or cliché wellness aesthetics.
